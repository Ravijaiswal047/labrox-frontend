# LabRox Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment
As contributors and maintainers of LabRox Enterprise LIMS, we pledge to foster an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Standards & Expectations
- Demonstrate empathy and kindness toward other people
- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback
- Accept responsibility and apologize to those affected by our mistakes, learning from the experience
- Focus on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community and clinical software integrity

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Harassment in any form, public or private
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Any conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement & Reporting
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project team at `security@labrox.io`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
